# Thanks

Network Diagnostics stands on the work of the open-source community.
This page is the project's public thank-you — if your project belongs
here and we missed you, open an issue and we will fix it.

## Ideas and engineering patterns

- **[HProxy](https://github.com/hproxy-com/proxy-all-in-one-tool)** —
  the paste-and-test shape, live streaming results, and the warning
  pattern for proxies that intercept HTTPS.
- **[ProxyReaper](https://github.com/rtulke/proxyreaper)** — the async
  checking benchmark: high concurrency, GeoIP caching, structured exports.
- **[Networking Toolbox](https://github.com/Lissy93/networking-toolbox)**
  by Alicia Sykes — proof that a hundred small tools belong in one clean,
  offline-first window. Our domain-health tab follows its example.
- **[mubeng](https://github.com/mubeng/mubeng)** — proxy checking and
  rotation done right, in Go.
- **[monosans/proxy-scraper-checker](https://github.com/monosans/proxy-scraper-checker)**
  — the generator behind one of our best sources; its
  check-before-you-publish discipline is the standard we copy.

## Data sources

Every list in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) is fetched at runtime from its
publisher and remains their work: TheSpeedX, monosans, ProxyScrape,
stormsia, HProxy, proxifly, jetkai, databay-labs, iplocate, roosterkid,
clarketm, hookzof, proxmint, relayglass, zloi-user (hideip.me), vakhov,
mmpx12, Zaeem20 — plus two projects that publish tools and registries
rather than fetchable lists: **[gfpcom/free-proxy-list](https://github.com/gfpcom/free-proxy-list)**
and **[fahimscirex/proxybd](https://github.com/fahimscirex/proxybd)**.

## Foundations

- The **judge/echo** pattern (popularized by httpbin) that makes
  anonymity grading possible.
- **[ip-api.com](https://ip-api.com)** — free geolocation lookups used
  for the residential/datacenter classification.
- The **Tauri** and **Rust** communities, whose tooling lets one small
  codebase ship native apps everywhere.
